How a turn works

  1. The pronouncer says your word. Ask for the definition, a sentence, the origin, or a repeat as often as you like.
  2. Tap the mic, say the word, spell it one letter at a time, then say the word again.
  3. When you say the word the second time, your answer is judged. If the mic misheard a letter, tap Appeal to try again.
