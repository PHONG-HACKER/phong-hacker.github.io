Dirt Pit Tycoon

You start with nothing but pebbles. Throw them into the pit for cash, then buy droppers that pour grains onto conveyor belts, upgrade your dirt to rarer ores. Fill the pit to rebirth: you get 5% faster and the pit gets deeper. It never ends.
